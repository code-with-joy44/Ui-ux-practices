Messi retires from international football with goal and assists as Argentina defeat Benin

Updated : 7 October 2026, 10:32 • FOOTBALL

AFP, Buenos Aires


Lionel Messi waves goodbye to his adoring fans. Photo: Reuters

Lionel Messi brought the curtain down on his 21-year international career on Tuesday, bidding farewell to adoring Argentina fans in an emotional final appearance for the former world champions in Buenos Aires.

In a perfectly scripted finale, the 39-year-old widely regarded as the greatest football player in history signed off with his 126th international goal in front of 85,000 supporters as Argentina cruised to a 3-0 victory over Benin.
